  Flip-flop dynamic power:
    433 FFs × 5 fW/Hz/FF × 100 MHz × 0.1 = ~21.7 µW

  Combinational dynamic power:
    2442 cells × 2 fW/Hz/cell × 100 MHz × 0.1 = ~48.8 µW

  Estimated leakage (433 FFs + 2442 combo × 0.2 µW avg per cell):
    ~575 µW

  Total estimated power: ~650 µW (0.65 mW)

This is within the expected range for a small accelerator on sky130.
Actual power from OpenLane (with activity factors from simulation) will
be more accurate and is planned for M4.
