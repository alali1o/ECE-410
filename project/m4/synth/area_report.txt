========================================================================
DOMINANT AREA CONTRIBUTOR
========================================================================
  The 281 flip-flops contribute ~2,108 μm² (~37% of estimated total).
  The dominant submodule by gate count is the SPI slave shift registers
  (16-bit RX, 8-bit TX, 5-bit bit counter = 29 bits of state registers)
  and the compute_core accumulator (32-bit register updated each cycle).

  For production, the weight SRAM (251 KB) would dominate; it is not
  synthesisable by Yosys — a foundry SRAM macro would be instantiated.
